The Pacto delivery promoter is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

It moves a Stage's pointer only through Operations, and it only ever writes
spec.ref.digest on the OCIRepository a Stage names. Label that OCIRepository
delivery.pacto.trianalab.io/apply-ref=<stage> so admission protects the pointer.

Bind {{ include "pacto-delivery.fullname" . }}-agent to your agents' own service
accounts and {{ include "pacto-delivery.fullname" . }}-approver to the people who may
approve. Then:

  pacto delivery plan --namespace <ns> --stage <stage> --to sha256:<digest>
{{- if .Values.recorder.enabled }}

The recorder writes a signed record of every concluded operation to
{{ .Values.recorder.streamsRepository }} and to the versions it touched. Check that
records are being written:

  kubectl get stages -A -o custom-columns=NAME:.metadata.name,RECORDS:.status.conditions[?(@.type=="RecordStoreReady")].reason
{{- end }}
