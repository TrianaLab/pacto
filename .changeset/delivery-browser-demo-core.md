The browser demo plays the payments story: `pacto init` pactifies a plain
service, then a safe change ships on its own, a bad release is rolled back, a
breaking change asks only the teams whose declared range it breaks and every
step reads back as a signed record. It is simulated and says so: plans, checks
and records come from Pacto's own code on fixed data, records are signed with a
key made in the tab and nothing reaches a cluster. A simulated sign-in and a
person switch show who may decide. A dashboard host may now handle sign-in
itself through `window.__pactoSignIn` and ask the views to read again with a
`pacto:refresh` event. The demo's Wasm is about 4 MB to download.
