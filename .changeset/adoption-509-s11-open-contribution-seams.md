Any tool, such as an internal developer platform, feeds Pacto inferred services, their calls and running targets through two signed formats it already reads, with no Pacto release and no Go code. A producer pushes one bundle per service to `<registry>/pacto-inferred/<producer>/services`, signed with its evidence key through the new `pacto push --sign-key`, and reports targets as signed EvidenceSets to the Evidence Server. The trust config grants a producer its own repository through an existing `allowedContractRepos` entry, and `GET /api/evidence/v1/producers` advertises the grant with the producer's public keys. The dashboard, `pacto fleet` and the MCP fleet tools show each producer as one row in Detected providers with its own status and `asOf`, a contributed service as inferred and contributed by its producer, and a target as reported by it with its evidence age. A contributed service never satisfies a range, never clears a gate and never reads Compliant; a silent producer and an old record read stale; a contract of the same name wins. The organization index refuses any bundle under `pacto-inferred`. The new [Write a source](https://pacto.run/latest/write-a-source/) page shows a whole producer in one short script. No contract field, `pactoVersion`, route, MCP tool, Helm value, RBAC rule or CRD changes.
