Deliver to a member through the registry. On a hub, `pacto delivery plan --service <svc> --environment <env> --to sha256:... --registry <registry>` plans a promote over the approved members and names its placement (`policy` from a signed placement rule, `unconfirmed` from the environments members take requests for, `advertised` with `confirmedBy` once a person signed) and each member's state; `pacto delivery propose ... --confirm` signs one member request per placed member keyless as you and pushes it to that member's request stream in `<registry>/pacto-targets`. The hub writes nothing into a member. Each member's operator verifies the request under its own approver signers, checks your grant there with its own SubjectAccessReview, its allowlist and that the Stage did not move, then raises one promote as an agent on your behalf that its own gates and approvals decide; every refusal and conclusion is an outcome record signed with the member's installation key, shown on the hub's plan, the Members block of the Environments board, `pacto delivery refusals --registry` and `pacto_delivery_refusals` (`check: member`). `pacto delivery approve --intent <record> --registry <registry>` approves a held member promote with no trust file. New chart values `delivery.remote.enabled` (`auto`) and `delivery.remote.allow` (`services`, `namespaces`, `environments`, which only narrow); the operator gains a separate `<release>-member-intake` ClusterRole under delivery (Stage reads, and with member requests Operation create, SubjectAccessReview create and the identities ConfigMap) and a `<release>-member-recorder-key` ConfigMap. The join block gains `recorderKey`, `streamsRepository`, `streams`, `streamsCut` and `requests`, and a placement rule gains `clusters` (member uids); a managed Stage whose upstream rung runs on another member follows that member's Stage stream with its advertised recorder key. The several-clusters delivery reference moves to its own page and leaves Experimental.
