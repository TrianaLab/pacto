A promote planned on a hub reads every approved member's signed snapshot beside the hub's own Pactos, through the same join reader the CLI and dashboard use, so a consumer in another cluster that does not accept the change asks its owners, and a member that went stale, a join list Pacto could not read or a cluster that is not a member yet asks a person to accept the unknown for a change that is not plainly non-breaking. The promoter derives the targets repository from `--registry`. A member's promoter runs with the new `--member` flag, which the chart passes whenever `hub.url` is set: it reads no joins and asks a person here for any change that is not plainly non-breaking, since consumers in other clusters are read on the hub.
